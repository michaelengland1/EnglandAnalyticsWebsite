Custom analytics

Custom analytics work built around your business.

We start with what you are trying to figure out, then build the reporting that answers it. This is the right route when your questions are specific to how your business runs, your data lives in more than one place, or nothing off the shelf lines up.

Who it is for

Businesses with real data and real questions that do not map onto a standard dashboard.

What this can include

  • Custom dashboards and business reporting
  • Data analysis on a specific question
  • Combining multiple data sources into one view
  • Excel and Power BI solutions
  • SQL and data preparation
  • KPI development and definitions
  • Other analytics projects, scoped individually

Pricing

Quoted per project. Scope varies enough that a fixed price would be dishonest in one direction or the other, so we talk about what you need first and I give you a number before any work starts.
