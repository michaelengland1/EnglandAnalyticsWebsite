Warehouse Operations Dashboard

Track throughput, accuracy, and order flow across the warehouse without building a reporting system first.

What this dashboard helps you understand

  • How much volume moved through the operation and how that compares to last period
  • Where orders slow down between received and shipped
  • Whether picking and shipping accuracy is holding up as volume changes
  • How today or this week looks against the trend

Key metrics

  • Orders received, picked, and shipped over time
  • On-time shipment rate
  • Order accuracy and error rates
  • Units per hour and throughput by period
  • Open and backlogged order volume

Built on a standard operations data model so it maps onto most WMS exports.

What you receive

  • The dashboard, connected to your data and ready to refresh
  • Setup and the adjustments needed to fit your fields
  • Written instructions for using and updating it

Who it is designed for

Warehouses and fulfillment operations tracking volume and accuracy in spreadsheets or a WMS export.

How implementation works

  1. You tell me what you need

    A short form: which dashboard, what system your data comes from, and what your business does.

  2. I confirm it fits

    I look at your data situation and tell you honestly whether the template works, needs changes, or whether a custom project would serve you better. No charge for this part.

  3. Payment and data handoff

    Once we agree it is a fit, I invoice the $249 and you send an export or connection details.

  4. Setup and delivery

    I connect your data, make the adjustments needed to fit your fields, and send the finished dashboard with written instructions for using and refreshing it.
