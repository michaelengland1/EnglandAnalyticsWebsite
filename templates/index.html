How setup works

  1. You tell me what you need

    A short form: which dashboard, what system your data comes from, and what your business does.

  2. I confirm it fits

    I look at your data situation and tell you honestly whether the template works, needs changes, or whether a custom project would serve you better. No charge for this part.

  3. Payment and setup

    Once we confirm it's a fit, I send your invoice and begin setup.

  4. You're in control from here

    I connect your data, make the adjustments needed to fit your fields, and send the finished dashboard with written instructions for using and refreshing it. You maintain control of your ongoing business data going forward.
