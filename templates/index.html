What the price covers

Every template includes the same kind of implementation — the price scales with how many report pages the dashboard has, not with extra custom work.

Included

  • A dashboard template with your requested colors and logo.
  • Connecting it to your data source or export
  • The adjustments needed to fit your fields and naming
  • A working, refreshable dashboard delivered to you
  • Written instructions for using and updating it

Beyond the template

If you need metrics the template does not cover, extra report pages, several data sources combined, or calculations specific to how your business works, that is a custom analytics project instead.

I will tell you which one you need before you pay for anything.

How implementation works

  1. You tell me what you need

    A short form: which dashboard, what system your data comes from, and what your business does.

  2. I confirm it fits

    I look at your data situation and tell you honestly whether the template works, needs changes, or whether a custom project would serve you better. No charge for this part.

  3. Payment and setup

    Once we confirm it's a fit, I send your invoice and begin setup.

  4. You're in control from here

    I connect your data, make the adjustments needed to fit your fields, and send the finished dashboard with written instructions for using and refreshing it. You maintain control of your ongoing business data going forward.
